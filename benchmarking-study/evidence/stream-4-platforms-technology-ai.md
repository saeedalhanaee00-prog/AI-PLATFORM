## Stream A — Whistleblowing platform register (classification: Basic / Developed / Advanced / Leading Practice)

### Leading Practice
1. **ADNOC — Takallam** [O]. Anonymous ("provides the option of anonymity if required"); operated by SpeakUp, an independent Dutch company (Amsterdam); two-way communication in complete anonymity; tri-channel (web, app, phone); multilingual; open to "anyone inside or outside the ADNOC Group"; non-retaliation policy. Not evidenced: case number/status tracking, SLA, evidence upload, classification/triage. Why leading: only UAE platform evidenced with named independent foreign operator + full anonymity with two-way dialogue + tri-channel + multilingual + public access + dedicated non-retaliation policy.
2. **Etihad — Ethics Line** [O]. Anonymous option; independent third-party provider (Report-Key flow strongly indicates NAVEX EthicsPoint — corroborated inference, not stated verbatim); unique Report Key for status follow-up (case tracking + two-way); Compliance Officer triage; any-language reporting; open to employees, customers, suppliers, business partners, public. Not evidenced: SLA, evidence upload.
3. **FAB** [O]. Managed by named independent vendor (ethiX360 / StarCompliance, Rockville MD): FABListens@ethix360.com, portal FABListens.ipm.ae.starcompliance.com, WhatsApp channel, letters; "a unique case number will be assigned"; "completely confidential and anonymous"; anti-retaliation; country hotlines in different languages; published report taxonomy (fraud, bribery/corruption, regulatory misconduct, COI, human rights, data/security). Not evidenced: two-way beyond case number, SLA.

### Advanced
4. **Wajib (built by ADAA, adopted federally by UAEAA)** [G]. Launched May 2022; "leading secure digital reporting platform... to confidentially report any administrative or financial violations and corruption"; 2023: implemented across all UAE ministries and federal entities; open to all members of society; whistleblower protection stated; registration-based. Not evidenced: anonymity mechanics, tracking, languages, evidence upload, SLA.
5. **CBUAE portal (eservices.centralbank.ae/wb/)** [O]. Encrypted channel (June 2021); "anonymously submit your report by skipping the identification section"; confidentiality; evidence upload ("attachment of files and documents"); open to institutions, vendors, public; anti-retaliation. In-house hosted; no tracking/SLA evidenced.
6. **ADGM — AMANAH portal + Whistleblower Protection Regulations 2024** [O][L]. Confidential reporting with anonymity option; statutory regime (in force 5 July 2024; entity policies mandatory by 31 May 2025; Registrar sanctions incl. fines and licence action). Not evidenced: tracking, SLA, languages, upload.
7. **DFSA regime** [O][L]. Regime (not a platform): mandatory written procedures to report, assess, escalate; identity protection; anonymous reports protected; DFSA own channel; January 2025 Thematic Review with data (97% anonymity measures; 88% training; only 39% extend channels to third parties; weak post-report treatment monitoring).
8. **Dubai FAA — Nazaha + Decision 2/2025** [O][N]. Public channel for all members of society; FAA staff "strictly prohibited from disclosing" report data; statutory employment protection for reporters/witnesses (2025); no financial rewards; plus Financial Violations Reporting Platform for auditees (GITEX 2024). Not evidenced: anonymity option, tracking.
9. **FTA — Raqeeb** [G][L]. Monetary rewards (collected tax > AED 50,000, credibility/novelty conditions; FTA staff/relatives/tax agents excluded); confidentiality ensured; live since 2022. Only evidenced UAE programme with financial incentivisation. Platform mechanics not evidenced.
10. **Mubadala — Ethics & Compliance Helpline** [O]. Anonymous or named; strict anti-retaliation; extends to business partners; ISO 37002:2021 whistleblowing management certification (first in UAE per own annual review) + ISO 37301 + ISO 37001. User-facing features (operator, channels, languages, tracking) under-evidenced publicly.

### Developed
11. **MoF — Trust Voice** [O][G]. Secure, confidential; all stakeholders incl. society; anti-retaliation; web + MOFUAE app; aligned to 2018 federal anti-fraud Cabinet guidance. Not evidenced: anonymity mechanics, third-party hosting, tracking, two-way, SLA, upload.
12. **DGE — Tahadath** [O]. VERIFIED: NOT a general suggestions platform — "the official, secure platform for reporting fraud, misconduct, or ethical breaches involving internal DGE employees or suppliers and vendors who have direct engagements with DGE." Confidential; anti-retaliation ("without risk of retaliation, disciplinary action, or discrimination"); seven violation categories (report classification); legal alignment stated. Not evidenced: anonymity option, third-party hosting, tracking, two-way, SLA, evidence upload. Why Developed: dedicated secure channel with taxonomy and anti-retaliation, but lacking exactly the attribute set that ADNOC/Etihad/FAB evidence.
13. **Emirates Group — Speak Up** [O]. Internal online tool (groupworld / One dnata portal) + speakup@ mailboxes; confidential, anti-retaliation; employee-facing on the evidence. Anonymity/third party/tracking not evidenced.
14. **TAQA — helpline.taqa.com** [O]. Confidential and secure reporting; zero-tolerance for retaliation; Ethics & Compliance Office investigates; Audit Committee/Board oversight. (Caution: taqaglobal.com EthicsPoint page belongs to the Saudi TAQA.)
15. **Aldar** [O]. Third-party managed solution (EthicsPoint per Stream 1), anonymous reporting; substantiated fraud reported to the CEO. Vendor features not evidenced further.
16. **DP World — Speak Up NOW** [O]. All stakeholders; confidential; operator/mechanics not evidenced.
17. **Emirates NBD — Ethicsline** [O]. "Secure and anonymous reporting platform"; local-language reporting; in-house managed; annual training + policy sign-off; discloses report counts/outcomes in annual reports (transparency high-water mark).

### Basic
18. **ADCB**: form/email with supporting documents; 24/7 contact centre; confidential; no platform/anonymity/tracking evidenced.
19. **e&**: whistleblower@eand.com + hotline numbers; Speak Up commitment; Ethics and Compliance Department; mechanics not evidenced.
20. **Ministry of Energy & Infrastructure**: web channel for good-faith reports — Basic. **Dubai DET / Dubai Land Department**: published whistleblower protection policies — Basic/Developed.
21. **Dubai Department of Finance (dof.gov.ae)**: No sufficient public evidence identified of a whistleblowing portal; Dubai routing runs via FAA Nazaha.

## Stream B — Technology & AI register (status: Implemented / Pilot / Announced)

1. **ADAA — Al Mersad AI audit platform** — Implemented (successive releases + operational demonstrations; sourcing is official announcements). Built with Presight.ai (G42); GITEX 2023 debut; "AI and big data analytics... anomaly detection... risk assessment based on data pattern recognition"; Al Mersad 2.0 + Smart Uploader (GITEX 2024, "intelligent automatic data mapping"). No independent audit-outcome evidence. [G][N]
2. **ADAA — updated Internal Audit Rules mandating data analytics across Abu Dhabi government internal audit units** — Implemented (rules issued). Directly relevant precedent for an Abu Dhabi department. [G]
3. **FTA — AI-driven tax administration** — Implemented at service layer (official: AI in "data analysis, risk assessment, request processing and automation"; EmaraTax; FTAGPT internal staff assistant; Q1 2026 refund processing 1m10s). Fraud-detection mechanics (network analysis, e-invoicing cross-matching) are advisor-sourced — do not credit as verified practice. [N/G][L][V]
4. **CBUAE — SupTech + Enterprise Data Management (FIT Programme, with Accenture)** — Pilot / in implementation (announced April 2024, two-year programme; ~85% complete per one report). "Analytics, automation, and AI-driven decision-making through a single unified supervision portal." Do NOT credit as implemented practice. [O][N]
5. **DGE — TAMM 3.0 AI Assistant incl. AI incident intake** — Implemented (launched October 2024; "over 700,000 conversations and resolved more than 1.4 million cases"; Arabic/English, voice and chat). Directly relevant NLP evidence: "Users can capture a photo or video of any incident, the AI extracts relevant details and automatically submits the report... routed to the appropriate Abu Dhabi Government entity" — a live AI classification-and-routing pipeline for general incidents (not integrity reports). Richest write-ups are vendor case studies (Microsoft, LangChain) corroborated by government-reported figures. [V][N]
6. **MOHRE — AI-supported labour dispute resolution** — Implemented per official claims ("settled 98.6% of UAE labour disputes amicably in early 2026, using AI-powered digital services"; 22+ languages); model detail thin — treat depth cautiously. [N]
7. **Dubai FAA — GITEX 2024 digital audit services** — Implemented (launched): Al Mo'ashir Dashboard consolidating operations/compliance/performance/financial data and "instances of financial and administrative misconduct"; Financial Violations Reporting Platform; Legislative Platform. AI not claimed. [O][N]
8. **Named GRC/case-management vendor implementations by UAE government entities** — No sufficient public evidence identified (beyond ADAA–Presight and FAB's ethiX360/StarCompliance).
9. **NLP/predictive triage of whistleblowing reports specifically** — evidenced NOWHERE in the UAE public domain — a defensible white-space claim. (TAMM incident routing and MOHRE are the closest analogues.)
10. **Dubai AI-based fraud detection in government services** — No sufficient public evidence identified (vendor marketing only).
11. **AI governance context**: National AI Strategy 2031 (2017), AI Office; national AI Ethics Principles and Guidelines (December 2022) with voluntary self-assessment; Dubai Ethical AI Toolkit (January 2019; adopted by Dubai Police, RTA); federal Artificial Intelligence and Data Authority established 2026 (cabinet level). [G][L]

## Headline for the benchmarking narrative
UAE leading practice concentrates where an independent third party operates the channel with anonymity, two-way dialogue, multilingual multi-channel access and tracking (ADNOC/SpeakUp, Etihad, FAB). Abu Dhabi government's own reference points are Wajib and ADGM's statutory regime; Mubadala's ISO 37002 certification is the standout assurance move. On AI: the only oversight-specific implemented system evidenced is ADAA's Al Mersad; AI triage of whistleblowing reports specifically is evidenced nowhere in the UAE public domain.
