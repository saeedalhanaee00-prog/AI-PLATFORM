## 1. ADNOC Group — Evidence: STRONG
- Practice: Group-wide third-party whistleblowing platform "Takallam"; Group Code of Conduct (2016, 2020, 2022 editions public); Supplier & Partner Code of Ethics; whistleblowing & non-retaliation policy (disclosed at ADNOC Distribution).
- Evidence: Takallam is "an Ethics Helpline run by SpeakUp, an independent third party, on behalf of the ADNOC Group"; "a confidential, 24 hour third party ethics helpline platform"; "enables reports to be made with total anonymity and in your preferred language"; online or by telephone in a number of languages; ADNOC "encourages anyone inside or outside the ADNOC Group to raise business ethics and integrity concerns"; "The ADNOC Group strictly prohibits retaliation of any kind." Gifts/hospitality (2022 Code): inappropriate where "no legitimate business reason", "disproportionately lavish", "offered to improperly influence" (no monetary threshold publicly stated). COI: "A Conflict should be avoided if possible. Where it is not possible... you must declare it." ADNOC Distribution: "100% of employees completed the anti-bribery and corruption training" (group).
- Sources: takallam.ae · adnoc.ae/en/our-story/code-of-conduct · adnoc.ae 2022 Group Code of Conduct PDF (.ashx) · Supplier Code of Ethics PDF · adnocdistribution.ae sustainable-governance.
- Status: Implemented in practice. ISO 37001: No sufficient public evidence identified.

## 2. Mubadala Investment Company — Evidence: STRONG
- Practice: Ethics & Compliance Office; certified to ISO 37001:2016 (anti-bribery), ISO 37301:2021 (compliance), ISO 37002:2021 (whistleblowing management) — the certification trio is the standout regional practice. Group Code of Conduct + Business Partner Code of Conduct; mandatory annual training.
- Evidence: "Mubadala becomes only the fourth Company globally to be awarded ISO 37301-2021" (own news); 2024 Annual Review: "recently received ISO 37002:2021 Whistleblowing Management System certification and was the first in the UAE to receive this certification, and also recertified in ISO 37301:2021"; Code: mandatory annual eLearning; suspicious gifts/hospitality/payments reported immediately to the E&C Office; Code of Conduct: "Mubadala's Ethics & Compliance Office is responsible for investigating and remediating alleged violations".
- Sources: mubadala.com ISO 37301 news page · annual2024.mubadala.com/en/about-mubadala · Business Partner Code of Conduct PDF · Group Code of Conduct PDF (leaseplan.com mirror — prefer mubadala.com original) · abacgroup.com (vendor, corroboration only).
- Status: Implemented (certifications awarded + recertified). Gaps: channel operator name and gift thresholds — No sufficient public evidence identified.

## 3. ADGM — Evidence: STRONG (regulatory benchmark)
- Practice: Whistleblower Protection Regulations 2024 — first comprehensive whistleblowing regime in Abu Dhabi jurisdiction; in force 2024; full-compliance deadline 31 May 2025 (passed — obligations live).
- Evidence: protected disclosures = "good faith reports of knowledge or reasonable suspicions" of contravention of "an ADGM law, regulation or rule" or "money laundering, fraud or any other financial crime"; anonymous good-faith reporting protected; immunity from "civil or contractual liability, employment termination, or any form of detriment"; entities "must have arrangements in place by 31 May 2025"; FSRA-regulated entities/DNFBPs/DLT Foundations need "written policies and procedures", records ≥ 6 years; employers must "assess and escalate any concerns" and "protect the identity of the whistleblower"; Registrar may "issue censures, impose financial penalties, or suspend or withdraw licenses".
- Sources: adgm.com whistleblowing obligations page · assets.adgm.com Consultation Paper No 2 of 2024 PDF · en.adgm.thomsonreuters.com ADGM1547 rulebook PDF · law-firm corroboration (Addleshaw Goddard, Hogan Lovells, HSF Kramer — secondary).
- Status: Implemented.

## 10. Department of Government Enablement (DGE) — "Tahadath" — Evidence: MODERATE
- Practice: Tahadath — official public reporting platform for DGE employees and engaged suppliers/vendors, documented on dge.gov.ae with a defined violation taxonomy.
- Evidence: "Tahadath is DGE's official, secure platform for reporting fraud, misconduct, or ethical breaches involving internal DGE employees or suppliers and vendors who have direct engagements with DGE"; "full confidentiality and without risk of retaliation, disciplinary action, or discrimination"; "zero-tolerance policy toward misconduct"; seven violation categories publicly listed incl. "Financial Violations (data manipulation, unapproved payments, misuse of accounts, revenue negligence), Procurement Fraud (bribery, kickbacks, policy manipulation, contract breaches), Abuse of Assets and/or Power, Technology Misuse". Related: Abu Dhabi Government Digital Strategy 2025-2027 ("AI-native government").
- Sources: dge.gov.ae/en/tahadath · dge.gov.ae/en/about-dge/faqs · dge.gov.ae digital strategy news page.
- Status: Implemented. Note: the study's "self" baseline; one of the few Abu Dhabi government departments with a public named channel and published violation taxonomy.

## 11. Department of Health Abu Dhabi — Evidence: MODERATE (regulator-mandated model)
- Practice: "Whistleblowing in Healthcare Facilities Policy" — sector regulator requiring whistleblowing arrangements in all licensed facilities, with sanctions.
- Evidence: "Reporting of wrongdoing could be through open, confidential or anonymous channels"; wrongdoing broadly defined; providers expected to "institutionalize a culture of integrity and ethics... without fear of retaliation"; DoH "may impose disciplinary actions and monetary sanctions for any breach of requirements under the whistleblowing policy".
- Sources: doh.gov.ae policy PDF (.ashx) · doh.gov.ae/en/resources/policies.
- Status: Implemented. With ADGM, the second Abu Dhabi example of a regulator mandating whistleblowing systems on its regulated population.

## Cross-cutting observations (evidence-based)
1. Third-party hotline is the norm among mature entities: SpeakUp (ADNOC Takallam), NAVEX EthicsPoint (Aldar, TAQA, Masdar Kallemna), unnamed third parties (Etihad, AD Ports). Government departments (DGE, ADPF) run in-house portals.
2. Case tracking / two-way communication explicitly evidenced only at Etihad (Report Key).
3. Certification ladder: Mubadala (37001 + 37301 + 37002) » Public Funds Prosecution (37001) » others (none evidenced).
4. Open-to-public reporting: ADNOC, ADQ, Etihad, AD Ports; DGE Tahadath scoped to employees + engaged suppliers.
5. Gift thresholds in AED: no entity publishes numeric thresholds; bright lines found: Aldar ("cash never allowed"; Audit Committee pre-approval), ADNOC qualitative criteria.
