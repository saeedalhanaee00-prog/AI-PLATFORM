# Evidence Register — Stream 2: UAE Federal and Dubai Entities

Date accessed for all items: 2026-09-04. Evidence tier: verified via official-domain search extraction (direct fetch blocked by environment egress policy); quotes are literal extractions from the cited official URLs, corroborated across 2+ queries where possible; re-confirm each URL from a normal connection before publication.

## 1. UAE Ministry of Finance — "Trust Voice" — STRONG
- Dedicated whistleblowing e-service + "Whistleblower" digital service in the MOFUAE mobile app. "A secure and confidential communication channel that can be accessed by all stakeholders associated with the Ministry (employees, customers, suppliers, partners, society, etc.)" to "submit their whistleblowing reports with good intentions about any corruption violations... without any fear of retaliation." Aligned with 2018 federal anti-fraud Cabinet guidance. Reportable: theft, damage to property/assets, money laundering, forgery, bribery, embezzlement, safety risks, legal violations, cover-ups. "The law protects the whistleblower and ensures that his or her identity will be anonymous."
- Scope nuance: Trust Voice covers violations in the Ministry of Finance itself — the cross-federal platform is UAEAA's Wajib.
- Case tracking / two-way communication: No sufficient public evidence identified.
- Sources: mof.gov.ae/en/services/trust-voice/ · u.ae corruption reporting page. Status: Implemented.

## 2. UAE Accountability Authority (federal SAI) + Wajib — STRONG
- Legal basis: Federal Decree-Law No. 56 of 2023 (19 December 2023) — "the supreme authority for financial control, auditing, integrity and transparency," replacing the Supreme Audit Institution (Federal Law 8/2011); "reports directly to His Highness the President." Functions include "preventive measures, controls for administrative investigations, financial disclosures, identifying strong mechanisms to protect whistleblowers and witnesses... initiatives to recover public funds."
- Reporting channel guarantees (official violations page): confidentiality "without obligating them to disclose their identity"; "shall not disclose any information with other entity without a prior consent of the whistleblower"; leniency — reporting before disclosure "may be a reason to exempt them from penalty"; open to "all individuals... whether they are federal government employees or clients or suppliers."
- Wajib (wajib.gov.ae): secure digital reporting platform developed and launched by ADAA in May 2022, then "implemented... across all UAE ministries and federal entities" — "an official channel for the general public to report misconduct in public funds at the federal level" (WAM, 8 June 2023). Registration required; explicit reference-number/two-way feature: No sufficient public evidence identified.
- Context: UNODC–UAE US$5.4m agreement (May 2021) funding the "Abu Dhabi Declaration Programme".
- Sources: uaeaa.gov.ae violations page and news N19122023 · uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2174 · mediaoffice.abudhabi Wajib rollout · wam.ae 1395303166827 · u.ae report-corruption page · wajib.gov.ae. Status: Implemented (per-entity rollout depth unverified).

## 3. FAHR — Federal Code of Ethics, HR Law 49/2022, discipline — STRONG
- Cabinet-adopted "Code of Professional Conduct and Ethics for the Public Service in the Federal Government" (uaelegislation entry 2112, updated 1 July 2023). Principles: "Confidentiality, Compliance, Integrity, Diversity and Inclusivity, Justice and Equality, Conflict of Interests and Disclosure, Adherence to Ethics of Using Social Media Platforms, Optimal Utilization of Government Resources, and Gifts and Bribes."
- COI: "avoid any conflict of interest, actual or potential"; employee facing COI "has the right to request a transfer"; unresolved cases go to the entity's HR department.
- Gifts: "Public servants or any of their family members up to the fourth degree may not accept any gifts, hospitality or services from whomsoever if this results in any form of obligation, or have a direct or indirect impact on the objectivity of job performance."
- Duty to report: "Public servants must report any irregularities they become aware of to uphold public interest."
- Discipline: Federal Decree-Law 49/2022 + Executive Regulations — penalties up to dismissal; Grievances Committee and Objections Committee; entity HR imposes disciplinary actions.
- Sources: fahr.gov.ae code page and PDFs (5b6ab7ec.pdf, 3d5f9dba.pdf, 674c44b4.pdf, 2024/03/2676aa27.pdf) · uaelegislation.gov.ae/1594 and /2112 · u.ae summary PDF. Status: Implemented (binding on federal entities).

## 4. Central Bank of the UAE — STRONG
- Own encrypted whistleblowing portal (June 2021): eservices.centralbank.ae/wb/ — "for internal and external stakeholders to voice concerns about misconduct or policy violations made by its contractors, representatives and employees"; "anonymously raise concerns... without any fear of reprisals"; covers "corruption, fraud, undisclosed conflicts of interest and ethical violations by CBUAE personnel"; phone and written reports; attachments supported. Scope: CBUAE itself, not complaints against banks.
- Enforcement: proportionate AML/CFT sanctions, "enforcement policy" considering seriousness/deliberateness; risk-based examinations with "detailed testing and extensive interviewing of key staff."
- Statutory reporter protection: Article 15 AML-CFT Law + Article 17.3 AML-CFT Decision protect institutions and staff for good-faith STR reporting; failure to file an STR is a federal crime.
- Rulebook requirement forcing licensed institutions to run internal whistleblowing programmes: No sufficient public evidence identified.
- Sources: eservices.centralbank.ae/wb/ · centralbank.ae/en/cbuae-amlcft · rulebook.centralbank.ae STR guidance · press corroboration (The National, Gulf Business, June 2021). Status: Implemented.

## 5. CMA (formerly SCA) — MODERATE
- Rename: Federal Decree-Laws 32/2025 and 33/2025, effective 1 January 2026 — SCA reconstituted as Capital Market Authority (uaecma.gov.ae live). Cite as "CMA (formerly SCA)".
- Governance reporting channel: "gives employees, customers, suppliers, and strategic partners the opportunity to report actions that contradict SCA's principles of Good Governance" — sca.gov.ae/en/reach-us/submit-report.aspx.
- Transparency: public open data on violations and violators (fines up to AED 10m) — sca.gov.ae/en/open-data/violations-and-violators.aspx.
- Anonymity/tracking/anti-retaliation details: No sufficient public evidence identified. Status: Implemented.

## 6. Federal Tax Authority — "Raqeeb" — STRONG
- Monetary-reward whistleblowing programme for tax violations/evasion (April 2022) — the UAE's only verified paid-bounty government scheme. "FTA... will verify the reports and grant monetary rewards to informants." Conditions: information "credible, accurate, and previously unknown to the FTA"; collection exceeding AED 50,000; objections/appeals exhausted. Excluded from rewards: FTA employees and relatives to the fourth degree, tax agents. Covers VAT, Excise, Corporate Tax.
- Exact anonymity wording: No sufficient public evidence identified (reward mechanics imply identified informants).
- Sources: u.ae Raqeeb page · PwC ME tax alert. Status: Implemented.

## 7. National frame — UNCAC and architecture — MODERATE-STRONG
- UNCAC signed August 2005, ratified February 2006, adopted through Federal Decree No. 8 of 2006.
- u.ae official pages list operative channels: Wajib (UAEAA), MOFUAE app Whistleblower service, entity-level services.
- Penal Code: bribery participant who reports "before it is discovered would not be subject to penalty" (article number varies across secondary sources — see Stream 3).

- Published UAE National Anti-Corruption Strategy document or standing committee with public terms: No sufficient public evidence identified.

## 8. Dubai Financial Audit Authority — Nazaha + Decision 2/2025 — STRONG (best emirate-level public-sector benchmark found)
- Nazaha platform: "a safe channel for all members of society to report financial and administrative violations committed in the entities subject to audit by the Financial Audit Authority... secure and confidential"; FAA employees "strictly prohibited from disclosing, sharing, or revealing any data or information contained in these reports under any circumstances." faa.gov.ae/en/nazaha.
- Decision No. 2 of 2025: protection of public employees who report/disclose financial or administrative violations or cooperate with FAA — "keeping the whistleblower's identity and employment information confidential, requesting that the audited entity refrain from taking action against the employee, and ensuring that the employee is not subjected to discrimination or unfair treatment." Coverage: Dubai government departments, public agencies/institutions, special development zones and free zones, Dubai-government-owned companies and companies ≥25% government-owned.
- Anti-Fraud Framework (13 July 2023): "an integrated methodology that promotes the principles of transparency, integrity and combating financial and administrative corruption"; two platforms — Financial Violations Reporting Platform (for auditees) and Nazaha (community/clients).
- Sources: faa.gov.ae/en/nazaha (official) · Decision 2/2025 coverage: Gulf News, Khaleej Times, Arabian Business (February–March 2025) — confirm text at dlp.dubai.gov.ae · Anti-Fraud Framework: WAM-syndicated reports. Status: Implemented; anonymous-submission option and case tracking: No sufficient public evidence identified.

## 9. Dubai Economic Security Centre — STRONG (law) / MODERATE (operations)
- Law No. 4 of 2016, amended by Law No. 9 of 2019 (official texts on dlp.dubai.gov.ae). Mandate: "counter corruption and economic crimes... fraud, bribery, embezzlement, forgery and Public Funds abuse."
- Whistleblower protection: protection "against prosecution and disciplinary action provided their claims are true... and are made to the Dubai Centre for Economic Security"; disclosure to DESC not a breach of confidentiality agreements.
- Public reporting portal with anonymity/tracking features: No sufficient public evidence identified. Status: Implemented (statute in force).

## 10. DIFC / DFSA whistleblowing regime — STRONG
- Effective 7 April 2022 (announced 16 March 2022): first regulatory whistleblowing regime in the UAE. Scope: Authorised Firms, Authorised Market Institutions, DNFBPs, Registered Auditors.
- Requirements: "appropriate and effective policies and procedures to facilitate the reporting and assessment of regulatory concerns" and "measures to protect the identity of the Whistleblower and to protect them from suffering any detriment." DFSA intake treated confidentially, access "limited to a small number of expert DFSA staff."
- Thematic Review (published January 2025, fieldwork 2024) — hard benchmark data: "97% of respondents reported measures to ensure anonymity" but "many lacked systems to monitor whistleblowers' treatment after raising concerns"; "88%... provide training"; "only 39% extend these [channels] to third parties, such as ex-employees or service providers."
- Sources: dfsa.ae news + explainer + services.dfsa.ae portal · dfsaen.thomsonreuters.com 7 April 2022 notice · dfsa.ae thematic review page (+ Norton Rose Fulbright summary). Status: Implemented; supervisory follow-up ongoing.

## 11. DEWA and Dubai Customs — ISO-certified entities — STRONG (DEWA) / MODERATE (Customs)
- DEWA: ISO 37001 (November 2023), recertified August 2024 following "a comprehensive audit by Bureau Veritas"; ISO 37301:2021 renewed December 2025. Public "Anti-Bribery, Corruption & Fraud Policy and Procedures" and Code of Business Ethics PDF. Sources: dewa.gov.ae news pages (2023/11, 2024/08, 2025/12) and Code PDF (.ashx). Status: Implemented — strongest verified ISO-certified integrity management system in a UAE government entity in this stream.
- Dubai Customs: ISO 37001 via press-release syndication only — verify on dubaicustoms.gov.ae. RTA ISO 37001: No sufficient public evidence identified.
- ISO 37002 caution: it is a guidance standard — formal third-party certification is not available; UAE bodies selling "ISO 37002 certification" are marketing-only claims. (Mubadala's ISO 37002 claim in Stream 1 is the entity's own certification statement — reconcile the two in the study: treat as conformity attestation, wording to be verified.)

## 12. Emirates NBD — STRONG
- Group Policy Repository (July 2024) PDF: whistleblower policy for "suspected fraud, waste and abuse, corruption practices"; "reports can be made in the local languages used in the locations where Emirates NBD or its subsidiaries have operations"; "all employees undergo annual training and sign off the Whistleblower Policies and Code of Ethics"; anti-retaliation stated.
- Investigations governance (notable disclosure): "the Head of Fraud Prevention and Investigation assigns cases to a competent member under his supervision, who ensures that all reports are filed and evidence is maintained under secure and confidential conditions"; no investigator with "a vested interest in the outcome."
- Source: emiratesnbd.com group_policy_repository.pdf. Status: Implemented. Benchmark for multilingual channels + annual attestation + dedicated fraud investigation unit + disclosure of report counts/outcomes in annual reports.

## Corrections to the research brief established by evidence
1. Federal Decree-Law 23/2022 amends the Central Bank law — the UAEAA's basis is Federal Decree-Law 56/2023.
2. The federal cross-entity whistleblowing platform is UAEAA's Wajib (ADAA-built, federal rollout announced June 2023), not an MoF platform — MoF's Trust Voice covers MoF itself.
3. SCA has been the Capital Market Authority since 1 January 2026.
4. Dubai's operative public-sector whistleblower instrument is FAA Decision No. 2 of 2025 + Nazaha (not a "Dubai Finance whistleblowing portal").
