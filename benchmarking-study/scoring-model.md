## 2. Maturity scale

| Score | Level | Anchor definition |
|-------|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | Basic | Practice exists only as a general statement (code clause, generic email channel); no dedicated mechanism, owner, or published detail. |
| 2 | Developing | Dedicated mechanism exists (policy, channel, unit) but with limited disclosed capability: no anonymity/tracking, no methodology, no data. |
| 3 | Established | Documented framework with clear ownership, defined process, and at least one advanced attribute evidenced (e.g., anonymous reporting, case tracking, independent oversight, risk assessment cycle). |
| 4 | Advanced | Multiple advanced attributes evidenced and integrated (e.g., platform with tracking + anti-retaliation + supplier access; investigations with methodology + QA + referral protocols; risk-based COI review). |
| 5 | Leading Practice | The practice can serve as a reference model: comprehensive, independent, technology-enabled, with published evidence of operation (statistics, enforcement actions, certifications such as ISO 37001/37002) — not merely announced. |

## 3. Scoring rules

1. A dimension is scored only on evidence recorded in the evidence register (Section 26 of the report). No evidence → the dimension is marked "NE — Not Evidenced" and scored conservatively at 1, with the NE flag shown; an NE flag is a statement about public disclosure, not proof of absence of the practice.
2. Weighted score = Σ (dimension score / 5 × weight × 100). Every weighted total in the report is re-derived arithmetically before delivery and marked as calculated in session.
3. Public-domain benchmarking measures *disclosed* maturity. Government entities disclose less than listed/regulated entities; scores are therefore comparable within sectors more safely than across sectors. This caveat accompanies every scoring table.
4. Announced-but-not-evidenced technology receives no score uplift (per the Implemented / Pilot / Announced distinction).
